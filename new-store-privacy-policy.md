## 2. What information we collect

Depending on how you use the Store, we may collect:

- identity and contact details, such as your name, email address, telephone number and date of birth or age confirmation;
- account credentials and authentication information, including a password hash or third-party sign-in identifier;
- membership or approval information needed to operate a restricted-access store;
- delivery and billing details, including address, suburb, city, province, postal code and delivery instructions;
- quote, order, payment-status, refund, invoice, customer-service and courier-tracking records;
- messages, enquiries, complaints, uploaded documents and correspondence you send to us;
- technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, log data, pages viewed and security events;
- cookie and preference information, as described below; and
- information you voluntarily provide in a form, survey, review or support interaction.

We ask you not to submit health information, medical histories, information about sex life, criminal-record information, identity documents, or other special personal information unless we specifically request it for a lawful and documented purpose. If the Store’s proposed products or fulfilment process requires any such information, we will obtain specific legal advice and update this notice before collecting it.

## 3. How we collect information

We collect information directly from you when you register, apply for access, sign in, submit a quote, provide delivery details, upload proof of payment, contact support, or interact with the Store. We may also receive limited information from authentication providers, payment providers, fraud-prevention services, couriers, hosting providers and other service providers acting on our instructions.

Where permitted by law, we may receive information from a person authorised to act for you, a business customer, a payment provider, a courier, or a public source. We do not buy personal-information lists for account creation or use personal information for a new purpose that is incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected.

## 4. Why we use personal information

We use personal information only when we have a lawful justification, including where processing is necessary to:

- create and administer your account;
- review and manage an access or membership application;
- respond to enquiries and provide customer support;
- prepare, communicate and administer quotes;
- process accepted orders, payments, refunds, invoices and delivery;
- verify transactions, prevent fraud, protect the Store and investigate abuse;
- maintain business, tax, accounting, audit and transaction records;
- send service messages about your account, quote, payment, order or delivery;
- send optional direct marketing where permitted by law and where you have consented or another lawful exception applies;
- improve security, reliability, accessibility and performance;
- comply with a court order, legal obligation, regulator, law-enforcement request or professional-adviser requirement; and
- establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

We will not use your information for unrelated profiling or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects without a lawful basis, appropriate safeguards and any notices required by law.

## 7. Sharing and operators

We may share the minimum information reasonably required with:

- payment and fraud-prevention providers;
- courier, delivery and tracking providers;
- hosting, database, storage, email, authentication, customer-support and security providers;
- professional advisers, auditors and insurers;
- regulators, courts, law enforcement or other authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law; and
- a purchaser, successor or restructuring party if the business or relevant assets are transferred, subject to appropriate confidentiality and lawful-use controls.

A service provider processing information on our behalf is an “operator” under POPIA. We will require operators to process information only on our instructions, keep it confidential, apply appropriate security measures, assist with incidents and data-subject requests where appropriate, and return or delete information when the service ends unless retention is legally required.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your information with unrelated third parties for their own direct marketing without a lawful basis and, where required, your consent.
