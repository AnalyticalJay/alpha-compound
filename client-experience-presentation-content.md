# Anabolics Africa — Customer Experience & Sales Flow

## Cover

**Anabolics Africa**

**A premium members-only customer journey**

Client experience and sales-flow overview

## Slide 1

### Members-only access sets the tone

- A restricted-access storefront clearly positions the platform as premium and controlled.
- HD Labs and BP Pharma sit within one unified catalogue experience.
- The same journey is designed to feel clear on desktop and mobile.

## Slide 2

### Browse, filter, and build a quote

- Customers can browse the product catalogue by brand and category.
- Search and category controls reduce the effort required to find a product.
- Products are added to a quote rather than purchased before review.

## Slide 3

### Quote submission is deliberately focused

- The customer confirms delivery details and reviews the estimated total before submission.
- The quote moves to administrator review instead of creating an unapproved purchase.
- The customer receives a clear expectation for the next step.

## Slide 4

### The dashboard keeps every order understandable

- Quote progress makes submission, review, approval, payment, and completion visible.
- Customer order details bring products, messages, payment history, and delivery together.
- Payment-ready orders are highlighted so customers can act without searching.

## Slide 5

### Payment opens only after approval

- Customers select a secure PayFast checkout or PayFast Instant EFT after a quote is approved.
- Payment states show clear progress, failure recovery, and retry options.
- Sensitive payment actions remain inside the authenticated order experience.

## Slide 6

### Delivery tracking remains customer-facing

- Courier name, waybill, tracking link, delivery estimate, and progress remain visible after dispatch.
- Customers can copy the waybill in one click and follow the delivery timeline.
- The journey communicates Packed, Dispatched, In transit, Out for delivery, and Delivered states.

## Slide 7

### One connected journey from access to delivery

- Access the catalogue.
- Build and submit a quote.
- Receive approval, pay securely, and track delivery.

**Anabolics Africa combines controlled access with a clear, mobile-first customer flow.**
