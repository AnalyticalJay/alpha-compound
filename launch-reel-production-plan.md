# Anabolics Africa Vertical Launch Reel

## Approved Global Treatment

The reel is a **20-second, 9:16 vertical** launch asset for social media. Its audience is adults seeking a premium, membership-only e-commerce experience. The visual language is cinematic sports-luxury: charcoal-black gym environments, electric-blue interface light, controlled red accents, crisp high-contrast product-neutral UI, and a single clearly adult, muscular athlete wearing a fitted black training top. The reel will not depict real product packaging, dosage use, medical claims, or medical outcomes.

The sound direction is energetic instrumental electronic/hip-hop at approximately 116 BPM, with a deep pulse, crisp percussive transitions, and no voiceover. On-screen copy is limited to: **MEMBERS ONLY**, **ORDER IN SECONDS**, and **ANABOLICS AFRICA**.
