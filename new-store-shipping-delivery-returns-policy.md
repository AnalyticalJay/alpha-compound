## 7. Delivery address and receipt

The customer must provide a complete and accurate delivery address, recipient name, contact number and any access instructions. The customer should check the address before submitting the order.

If an address is incomplete or incorrect, we will contact the customer where reasonably possible. Additional delivery charges, re-delivery charges or cancellation consequences may apply only where lawful and where the customer caused the failed delivery.

The customer should inspect the external parcel packaging at delivery and record visible damage with the courier where possible. Do not refuse a parcel solely because the packaging has a minor cosmetic mark. If the parcel appears materially damaged, the customer should photograph it, note the damage on the courier record if possible, and contact **[support email]** promptly.

A customer should not sign a document confirming “received in good condition” if the customer has not had a reasonable opportunity to examine the parcel or if visible damage is present. A delivery signature does not remove statutory rights concerning defective, unsafe, incorrectly described or incomplete goods.

## 8. Failed delivery, refusal and re-delivery

A delivery may fail because nobody is available, the address is inaccessible or incorrect, the recipient cannot be verified, the parcel is refused, or the courier cannot safely or lawfully complete delivery.

We will work with the customer and courier to arrange a reasonable next step. Where the failure was caused by the customer, the next step may involve a reasonable re-delivery charge or cancellation handling, provided the charge is disclosed, proportionate and lawful. We will not charge a re-delivery fee where the failure was caused by our error, the courier’s error, a defective address label generated by us, or another circumstance for which the customer is not responsible.

If a customer refuses delivery because the goods are materially different from the accepted order, damaged, unsafe, defective or delivered at an unagreed time or place, contact **[support email]** immediately so the matter can be recorded and assessed under the applicable consumer rights.

## 9. Customer cancellation before dispatch

A customer may request cancellation before dispatch by contacting **[cancellations email]** with the order reference. We will confirm whether cancellation is possible and the refund due.

Cancellation is not guaranteed once the order has been accepted by the courier or has been delivered, but statutory rights remain available. If the Store cannot supply an accepted order, we will notify the customer and refund amounts due as required by law.

## 10. Cooling-off rights for electronic transactions

Where a statutory cooling-off right applies, the Store will honour it. ECTA provides a seven-day cooling-off right for certain electronic transactions involving goods, subject to the Act and its exclusions. The consumer may cancel without reason and without penalty, other than the direct cost of returning goods where applicable; refunds must be processed within the statutory period.[3]

ECTA’s cooling-off rule does not apply to every transaction. It may be excluded for specified categories or circumstances. The Store will not use a blanket “no returns” statement to remove a right that applies by law.

To exercise a cooling-off right, email **[returns email]** within the applicable period and provide the order reference. We will provide return instructions. The customer must take reasonable care of the goods and return them as required by law. We will not make a customer bear return costs where the law places that cost on the Store.

## 11. Goods that are defective, unsafe, damaged or not as ordered

Consumers have rights to receive goods that are reasonably suitable for their ordinary purpose, of good quality, in good working order, free of defects, and reasonably durable, subject to the CPA and applicable exceptions.[4]

If goods are defective, unsafe, damaged before delivery, incomplete, materially different from the accepted order, or not of the type or quality reasonably contemplated, contact **[returns email]** as soon as possible. Include:

- order number;
- name and contact details;
- description of the problem;
- photographs or other evidence where reasonably available; and
- the remedy requested.

The Store will assess the complaint and may request the goods for inspection. We will not require a customer to waive a statutory remedy or pay an inspection charge merely to exercise a protected right.

Under the CPA’s implied warranty provisions, a consumer may in specified circumstances return goods within six months after delivery when the goods fail to satisfy the statutory quality requirements. Subject to the Act and the circumstances, the consumer may choose a repair, replacement or refund, and the supplier bears the applicable return cost and risk.[5]

The Store may not exclude these rights by labelling goods “sale”, “clearance”, “no return” or “used” unless the relevant legal requirements are satisfied and the customer was clearly informed of a specific defect or limitation before the transaction.
